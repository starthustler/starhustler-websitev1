# StarHustler Reference Design Specification

The uploaded StarHustler page is the ground-truth visual and structural reference for this project. The implementation will preserve its scrolling narrative, dark-navy and electric-blue color blocking, confident Indonesian AI-education positioning, portrait-led editorial compositions, offset image clusters, and generous white-space rhythm. Fidelity to the reference takes precedence over alternative visual directions.

## Chosen Approach: Confident Learning Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indonesian edtech landing page design with editorial portrait direction and polished SaaS clarity.

### Core Principles
1. Alternate dense dark-navy campaign panels with crisp white content sections to maintain the reference's visual rhythm.
2. Use asymmetrical editorial compositions: portraits and overlapping image cards counterbalance information blocks rather than sitting in centered containers.
3. Present AI education as direct, practical, and human through candid professional portrait imagery and concise utility-forward copy.
4. Treat electric blue as the active signal for conversion actions, controls, and learning-progress cues.

### Color Philosophy
Dark navy establishes focus and credibility in the hero, benefit field, newsletter, and footer. White creates breathing room for detail-oriented learning content. Electric blue (#2C79FF) is deliberately reserved as the visible call-to-action and navigation signal. Black supports high-contrast editorial copy. These are the only colors used for the interface.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical editorial storyline made from alternating campaign fields. Each section has a distinct asymmetric focal composition: copy against a portrait, cards against whitespace, or a central human image framed by radial benefit points. The reading flow moves through practical value, social proof, programs, learning formats, FAQ, and conversion prompts.

### Signature Elements
- Soft rounded editorial photo frames that overlap and crop with intent.
- Thin constellation-like blue line motifs and orbit dots in dark panels.
- Circular blue check markers and compact icon tiles that make practical benefits scannable.

### Interaction Philosophy
Actions feel precise and reassuring: buttons press slightly, navigation remains easy to reach, FAQs expand without disorienting the reader, and mobile navigation condenses to a clear menu control.

### Animation
Use brief 160–240ms transform and opacity transitions only. Hovered cards lift 2–4px and blue buttons brighten minimally. FAQ content opens with a short reveal. Decorative orbit elements may drift subtly only when motion preferences allow; no looping or flashy animations interfere with reading.

### Typography System
Use **Poppins** for large, bold campaign headlines and **Plus Jakarta Sans** for compact supporting text and controls. Headlines use 700–800 weight and closely managed line length; body text uses 400–500 weight with generous line-height. Italics are used selectively for the hero's practical-learning assertion.

### Brand Essence
**StarHustler is the practical AI learning community for Indonesian solopreneurs who want to build smarter, faster businesses without theory overload.**

Personality: **direct, resourceful, encouraging**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are active and outcome-oriented; calls to action are confident, plain-spoken, and immediately useful. Microcopy sounds like a helpful peer who values the learner's time.

Examples: “Bangun bisnis yang berangkat dari ide, bukan dari bingung.” and “Pelajari. Praktikkan. Buktikan di bisnismu.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact white starburst paired with a bold custom-style StarHustler wordmark. The starburst references a spark of momentum and appears as a recognizable graphical device in the navbar, footer, and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Hustler Blue — #2C79FF**.

## Style Decisions

- Portraits are treated as a deliberate campaign set: confident Indonesian builders and mentors framed in varied editorial crops rather than interchangeable stock imagery.
- All navigation, class labels, FAQs, and conversion copy lead in Indonesian; English is reserved for established product and tool names.
- The starburst and thin blue orbit lines form a purposeful momentum system that marks learning paths, tools, and progress across the page.
