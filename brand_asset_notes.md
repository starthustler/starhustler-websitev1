# StarHustler Brand Asset Notes

## Supplied white horizontal lockup

The white lockup is a single horizontal composition with the brand emblem on the left and the Starthustler wordmark to its right. It is designed for dark-blue, black, or other sufficiently dark photographic backgrounds; it should not be used on white or pale backgrounds without a dark holding shape.

The source file dimensions are **1250 × 313 px**, an approximately **4:1 horizontal ratio**.

## Supplied full-colour horizontal lockup

The full-colour lockup uses the same emblem-plus-wordmark construction and the same 1250 × 313 px horizontal ratio. The emblem is built from two rounded, open geometric shapes: the upper form is dark blue and the lower form is vivid blue. A white upward arrow creates the negative-space focal point in the emblem. The wordmark is dark blue.

The wordmark itself is a custom geometric sans-serif treatment. It must be used only as supplied, rather than recreated with a substitute font or separately typeset. In normal copy, use the business name as **Starthustler**; use the supplied artwork as the authority for the visual lockup’s casing and letterform details.

## Supplied standalone emblem

The standalone emblem is a **625 × 625 px** square. It uses the same two-part dark-blue and vivid-blue geometry with a white arrow pointing up and to the right. Use it for social-profile avatars, favicon/app-icon foundations, compact thumbnails, favicons, and small-format applications where the full horizontal lockup would be unreadable. Do not rotate, recolour, redraw, separate its internal shapes, or add a stroke or shadow.
