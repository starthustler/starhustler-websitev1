# StarHustler ebook redesign brief

The section will promote one branded lead magnet rather than a generic stack of blank books. Its resource title is **Solopreneur Starter Kit**, with the explanatory line **Panduan Memulai Bisnis dengan AI** and author credit **Andre Elausta Tuwan**. The website heading and the ebook cover will use the same title and author.

The visual direction is a premium three-quarter product mockup: a blue hardcover guide and a dark tablet side by side, both carrying the same cover title. The cover uses StarHustler navy, vivid blue, a white upward star/arrow motif, and warm-white type. It must be crisp, text-safe, and placed on transparent or deep-navy space so it can sit directly within the homepage ebook section.
