# Typography legibility audit

## Finding

The original landing page used multiple 8–12 px styles for benefit descriptions, program cards, course metadata, FAQ rows and answers, ebook supporting copy, and footer details. These styles read too small at a standard desktop viewing distance.

## Repair applied

The repaired interface now uses a **13 px minimum** for visible supporting labels and metadata, **14–16 px** for practical support copy, **15 px** for FAQ questions and answers, and larger title, button, and card styles. The benefit-orbit layout was expanded to accommodate larger descriptions without crowding, and the program and course card dimensions were increased where the additional type needs space.

## Visual confirmation

The program, benefit, FAQ, ebook, closing CTA, and footer areas were reviewed at desktop browser scale. The mobile hero and responsive layout were also checked after the scale repair.
