# StarHustler page expansion plan

## Shared direction

Every page extends the **Confident Learning Editorial** system already established on the homepage: dark navy campaign fields, crisp white reading spaces, vivid-blue actions, Poppins display typography, Source Sans 3 utility typography, portrait-led compositions, and the constellation/orbit motif as a signal of progress.

## Page architecture

| Route | Page role | Primary sections | Conversion path |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/kelas` | Course catalogue | Course hero, three class pathways, learning rhythm, FAQ strip | Direct visitors toward an appropriate class and the newsletter resource. |
| `/company-training` | B2B training offer | Team-training hero, practical outcomes, delivery formats, enquiry panel | Prompt a discussion through an email enquiry. |
| `/komunitas` | Community experience | Community hero, ongoing member rhythm, six support modes, participation principles | Bring prospective members to the class catalogue. |
| `/tentang-kami` | Brand and founders | Mission hero, founder profiles, operating principles, partnership panel | Move visitors toward the community and training pathways. |
| `/blog` | Editorial hub | Insight hero, selected learning notes, topic filters, resource CTA | Help visitors explore a class or download the ebook. |

## Writing approach

New page copy will be practical, optimistic, and specific to the lived workflow of Indonesian solopreneurs. It will not invent testimonials, star ratings, student counts, customer logos, employer claims, course completion outcomes, or other unverified social proof.

## Route integrity

The header, footer, shared logo, all primary calls-to-action, and the footer Quick Link group will use real page routes. The homepage retains its own internal anchors only for its local hero and section journey.
